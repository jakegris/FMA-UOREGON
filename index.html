A student-run organization at the University of Oregon dedicated to bridging the gap between finance education and industry practice.

Bridge the Gap

We connect classroom finance theory with real-world practice, empowering students with the practical skills needed for a competitive industry.

Active Markets

Built by Students, for Students

Our mission is simple: empower University of Oregon students with the knowledge, skills, and network they need to succeed in finance. From weekly presentations on SaaS and AI stocks to site visits at companies like Tektronix, we're committed to making finance education accessible, engaging, and career-focused. We believe the best way to learn is by doing - analyzing real companies, meeting industry professionals, and thinking critically about global markets.

FMA members at our Tektronix corporate site visit

What We Do

Every week brings something new.

Guest Speakers

Learn directly from industry professionals. We've hosted speakers from Oregon Community Credit Union, Stifel, and Lincoln Financial Group who share real insights about careers in finance.

Member Presentations

Our members dive deep into topics like SaaS, AI stocks, energy, defense, healthcare, consumer discretionary, IPO outlooks, global economics, and emerging regulations.

Site Visits

Go beyond the classroom. We've visited companies like Tektronix in Beaverton, Oregon, giving members firsthand experience of how finance operates in the real world.

Why Join FMA?

Real Market Knowledge

Learn how to analyze stocks, understand market trends, and think like an investor through weekly presentations and discussions.

Industry Connections

Network with finance professionals from top firms and credit unions who can open doors to internships and careers.

Resume-Ready Skills

Build practical finance skills that stand out on resumes and in interviews - from financial modeling to sector analysis.

Real-World Site Visits

See finance in action with exclusive visits to companies like Tektronix, bridging theory and practice.

Leadership Opportunities

Take on leadership roles, organize events, and develop professional skills that extend far beyond finance.

Global & Macro Thinking

Explore emerging regulations, global economics, and macro trends shaping the future of markets.

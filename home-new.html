Built by Students, for Students

Our mission is simple: empower University of Oregon students with the knowledge, skills, and network they need to succeed in finance. From weekly presentations on SaaS and AI stocks to site visits at companies like Tektronix, we're committed to making finance education accessible, engaging, and career-focused. We believe the best way to learn is by doing - analyzing real companies, meeting industry professionals, and thinking critically about global markets.

What We Do

Every week brings something new.

Guest Speakers

Learn directly from industry professionals. We've hosted speakers from Oregon Community Credit Union, Stifel, and Lincoln Financial Group who share real insights about careers in finance.

Member Presentations

Our members dive deep into topics like SaaS, AI stocks, energy, defense, healthcare, consumer discretionary, IPO outlooks, global economics, and emerging regulations.

Site Visits

Go beyond the classroom. We've visited companies like Tektronix in Beaverton, Oregon, giving members firsthand experience of how finance operates in the real world.
